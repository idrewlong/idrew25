- **Scope:** whole repo on branch `chore/site-fixes` (2f74796 plus uncommitted Ask AI launcher change); pages on local dev (localhost:3001); performance, headers, robots/sitemap on production (idrewlong.com, one deploy behind).

## Executive summary
- **Performance is the weak spot:** Lighthouse mobile on `/` scores 54 (TBT 1379 ms, Speed Index 10.5 s), mostly the ~6.5 s first-visit preloader and first-load JS (Three.js, gtag). The blog post scores 95.
- **Accessibility:** axe found 1 violation across 16 runs; Lighthouse a11y, SEO and best practices are 100 on `/`. The real gap is that the mobile menu doesn't trap focus.
- **Security:** pass with lows. No critical, high or medium issues; no secrets in the client bundle; no XSS, SSRF or GROQ injection paths.
- **Code health:** the biggest risk is that there's no CI, so the new typecheck and 137 tests only run if someone remembers. The assistant's knowledge is a hand-kept copy of site content that has already drifted.
- **Needs Andrew:** confirm two pieces of personal copy the assistant and contact page repeat (see Content).
